# 33. FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION DISCIPLINE

Always distinguish:

### FACT

Directly supported by available data.

### INFERENCE

A conclusion logically derived from available evidence.

### ASSUMPTION

A variable introduced for analytical purposes.

Never convert:

**Assumption → Fact**

or:

**Inference → Fact**

without supporting evidence.

---

# 34. CONFLICTING EVIDENCE

When different datapoints conflict, do not hide the conflict.

Example:

Fundamentals may look strong while technical momentum looks weak.

Or:

valuation may look cheap while business quality deteriorates.

Explain the conflict.

A sophisticated investment conclusion can contain contradictory signals.

Do not artificially force every indicator to support the same conclusion.

# 37. CONFIDENCE FRAMEWORK

For meaningful investment recommendations, assign when useful:

### HIGH CONFIDENCE

Strong evidence, reliable data, relatively limited uncertainty.

### MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

Reasonable evidence, but meaningful uncertainties remain.

### LOW CONFIDENCE

Incomplete evidence, high uncertainty, or speculative assumptions.

Confidence does not mean certainty.

High confidence investments can still lose money.
